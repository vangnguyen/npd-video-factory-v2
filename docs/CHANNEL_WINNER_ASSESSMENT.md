# Channel winner assessment

Official-source collections now persist `winner-channel-assessment-v1` against their own immutable snapshot. Existing fixture assessments and historical records retain their previous algorithm. A winner remains a recommendation: no post deletion, paid-budget mutation or automatic publication occurs.

The policy is `winner-channel-policy-v1`. Default minimum is five distinct compatible posts and 500 views, 40% available configured weight, and scored retention or completion evidence. Median peer performance scores 50; twice median scores 100, capped at 100. When both current and median values are zero, the score is 50; a positive value against zero scores 100 without an infinite ratio. Default candidate/underperformance thresholds are 72/38. `ANALYTICS_WINNER_POLICY_JSON` supplies a bounded explicit policy with all ten factor weights; invalid policy fails processor construction with a fixed error before any read, without echoing raw configuration. The policy SHA256 is preserved with factors.

Eligible peers share workspace, exact account/profile/configuration binding, platform, provider, actual/mock transport flags, requested report interval, render profile, duration bucket and frozen niche. Source/scope evidence, published receipt and frozen feature digest/timeline binding must agree. Missing legacy feature context is not reconstructed from current tags. Up to 500 candidate rows, two latest observations per publication and 100 distinct remote posts are considered. Repeated publication records for the same remote post count once; a valid alternative observation survives an invalid one. Scan truncation is explicit. These statistics describe the bounded compatible set, not whole-account totals.

Each factor retains raw value, median, eligible peer snapshot references/count and policy digest. Engagement comparisons require the same supported interaction components. Missing metrics remain null. Recorded production-cost coverage and revenue collector provenance retain their existing limits. Same requested dates do not certify complete provider coverage or equal publication ages. Scores are descriptive comparisons, not causal proof that a hook or template improved performance.

View velocity uses changes between two cumulative counter observations separated by at least six hours by default. Negative counter changes, short intervals and period reports produce no growth estimate. It preserves delta views, collection interval, both history references and unknown provider update lag; actual publication age and normalized observation-window metrics remain null. Peer growth calculations and their earlier references are also retained. A strong counter-growth score cannot overcome missing retention/completion and turn insufficient evidence into a winner.

New render contexts retain full long annotations. The legacy analytic hook/visual columns have shorter limits; oversized hook labels remain null and visual labels use actual clip kinds, with unprojected fields and the original context digest explicit. The full annotations are preserved without truncation in render evidence. No live schema widening or data rewrite occurs.

Evidence is indexed in `docs/north-star/channel-cohort-evidence.json`. A deliberate transport flag simulation is identified separately. The cumulative rehearsal performs 24 TikTok-protocol mock reads over twelve collections, persists twelve observations and twenty-four unknown billed analytics costs, and remains insufficient_data. Native/channel UI integration, actual account/provider acceptance, independent AnalyticsProfile and full learning/personalization remain open. WINNER_DETECTION_READY remains NO.
