## Required RightsRecord

Each source or generated asset must validate against `schemas/rights-record.schema.json` and include:

- stable asset and rights-record IDs;
- source type, provider and source URL/object reference;
- owner/license/consent basis and permitted commercial/territory/duration scope;
- retrieval and terms-snapshot time;
- provider model/workflow/version for generated media;
- source, request and output hashes where collection is lawful;
- transformation lineage into the exact render;
- reviewer, expiry/retention and revocation status;
- `secret_recorded=false`.

## Hard-block rules

Unknown, ambiguous, expired, revoked, incompatible or missing rights must prevent approval and final
render use. Rights do not become valid because an asset downloaded successfully, was publicly
viewable, was generated by AI, or already appeared in a draft.

The final artifact manifest must reach 100% RightsRecord coverage. A timeline or asset mutation
invalidates the prior coverage result and approval. Rejected assets stay in the audit trail but are
removed from active timelines; no evidence is silently deleted.
